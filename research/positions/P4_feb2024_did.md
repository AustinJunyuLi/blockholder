# P4 — Control outcomes of the Feb-2024 acceleration: matched-control DiD with a bounded null

## 1. Object
**Bidder entry**: whether an initial-13D target receives a takeover bid (SC TO-T/DEFM14A/8-K) within 12 months of trigger. Secondary, descriptive: **takeover premium** on acquired deals (hand-collected ≤300).

## 2. Margin
The **window margin as a CHANGE**: initial 13D deadline ten calendar days→five business days, effective 2024-02-05 (`_institutional_sec_33_11253.md` §1, pp. 1, 10). Threshold and 13D/13G margins untouched.

## 3. Anchor
SEC Release 33-11253; first stage in hand (repo Fact 1): within-5-business-day share 35.7%→75.6%, median delay 7.0→5.0 business days (`empirical_feasibility.md` §1.1); Trivedi's DiD (+0.348, p=0.007) cited only as "a working paper reports the window bit" (t>3; `trivedi_2026_ssrn.md` §3 R2). Table 3 (p. 189) caps power: ~3% of non-corporate-action campaigns (≈7/year) constrained (§4.4).

## 4. Main result to be proved
**Statement:** the acceleration's effect on the 12-month bid hazard of 13D targets vs matched never-targeted firms, bounded and estimated; the companion model signs the per-campaign effect on constrained campaigns (shorter window⇒smaller filing stake⇒weaker λ_app⇒lower entry where stake–bargaining dominates certification). **Tool:** reduced-form DiD + one-shot accumulation/entry model (algebra, monotone comparative statics). **Proof route:** empirical leg: committed, re-runnable script on the data manifest; theory leg reuses the proposal's PROVED quadratic accumulation cost (`author_proposal_outline_2026.md` §3 R1) and draft_v2's PROVED λ=1−q(1−γ)ψ (D7); the entry sign holds under one stated condition. **Expected label:** ESTIMATED + PROVED-conditional. **Biggest technical risk:** power (§5, §9).

## 5. Empirical design
**Sample:** non-corporate-action initial 13Ds, 2022-01→2025-12 (~20% of 9,234 parsed originals, SEC Table 2 p. 181), in on-disk CRSP 2021–25; post leg Feb–Dec 2024, doubling after the parser fix (`empirical_feasibility.md` §1.2–1.3). **Identification:** DiD around 2024-02-05. **Control group:** never-13D firms matched 3:1 on size, Amihud, SIC2, quarter—*not* 13G: 13G filers disclaim control intent and 13D-vs-13G selection is plausibly rule-responsive (`trivedi_2026_ssrn.md` §7). **Bounded null (itself a result):** SEC Table 3 caps the constrained population at ~3%, so the population ATE on bid hazard is ≤~3 pp even if every constrained campaign dies—small footprint *because the tail is small*; no control group needed. **Confounds:** (i) EDGAR cut-off 5:30pm→10pm ET the same day (card §3, pp. 9, 11); Zeng's 1–13-calendar-day screen is non-neutral under a 5-business-day rule (`zeng_2026_ras.md` Q4, p. 1312 fn. 13)—we impose no delay screen, count in business days; (ii) anticipation (proposed 2022-03-10, adopted 2023-10-10)—pre ends at adoption; (iii) dated contaminants (T+1 2024-05-28; 13G deadlines 2024-09-30; XML 2024-12-18)—main post window Feb–May 2024, later months as extensions; (iv) BBJJ's second channel—faster disclosure alerts incumbents, who erect defenses (p. 28, Q20)—split on their low-trigger-pill moderator (pp. 28, 31) where pill data allow; else the bias is signed (defenses push toward a decline—an increase would be conservative); (v) selection into 13D—filer-mix diagnostics; estimates reduced-form. **Power/MDE:** ~300 campaigns/side; base hazard ≈17–18% (Greenwood–Schor p. 372, Table 6); matched-DiD MDE ≈9–10 pp (1yr/side), ≈6–7 pp pooling 2022–23 pre with 2024–25 post; premium leg (~50 deals/side, SD ≈0.38): MDE ≈15 pp, descriptive—Dass et al.'s premium DiD died here (<19 treated acquirers, fn. 27). **Placebo:** ≥500 reform dates 2021–2023. **Pre-trend:** quarterly bid-hazard event study 2022–23 by group; failure blocks causal language. **Parser validation:** 30-filing hand audit, per-quarter parse rates, business-day arithmetic asserted against `numpy busday`. **Run by December:** parser fix, outcome matching, matched DiD, bounded null, placebos, pre-trends; **only specified:** pill split, causal premium reading.

## 9. Self-assessed weakest point
Power. SEC tables cap the constrained population at ~7 campaigns/year; the matched DiD detects only ~6–10 pp on a ~17% base, the dose leg is weaker (proposal's MDE: 17–31 pp on ~3 exposed bids), and the premium leg cannot be causal at reachable n. The position survives only if the bounded null is itself a headline, not a consolation: "the aggregate footprint on control outcomes is small; the per-campaign estimate comes with honest intervals." If the tournament demands a headline *detection*, this family cannot promise one; it promises the cleanest *identified* statement on Feb-2024 × a control outcome, every checklist item answered on data in hand.
