# Literature sweep 2024–26, strand B — liquidity × 13D disclosure rule × control outcomes

**Agent:** sweepB (mechanical search/fetch, no judgment of what papers prove). **Date:** 2026-08-19.

**Scope.** Papers dated 2024–2026 (forthcoming/accepted/advance-access/published, or working-paper
series) that could compete with or neighbour: market liquidity × the Schedule 13D disclosure rule
(5% threshold and/or the 10→5 business-day filing window, SEC Release 33-11253, eff. 2024-02-05) ×
control outcomes (takeover premium, bidder entry, activism campaign success, 13D vs 13G choice,
toehold/stake accumulation).

(Celentano–Levine 2025, Johnson–Swem 2021, Cetemen–Cisternas–Kolb–Viswanathan 2026,

Polk–Buchheit–Riley–Stone 2024, Bishop–Fos–Jiang–Partnoy 2026, author's own proposal), everything

`research/lit_frontier-scan.md` (2026-08-18) already names (Burkart–Lee 2022, Burkart–Lee–Voss 2024,
