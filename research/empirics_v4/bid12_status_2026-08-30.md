   token of the core name.
2. **Acquirer-side rejection.** Salesforce's Slack 8-Ks (2021) describe
   "Merger Sub, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company". The
   firm-as-subsidiary target pattern carries a negative lookahead so that
   phrase cannot fire; the acquirer direction has its own pattern family
   ("Company completed the acquisition of …", "… agreed to acquire …", a
   party *becoming/surviving* as a subsidiary of the Company, etc.).
3. **Non-merger instruments.** Item 1.01 covers any material definitive
   agreement; debt/lease/supply 8-Ks that merely reference a merger are
   rejected by the instrument pattern family (rulebook §5, decision table
   row 4).

## 4b. Rulebook-violation repair (2026-08-30, pre-audit; second incident)

A fresh read-only review of the committed coder against the rulebook found
four violations still present after §4's fix, plus three smaller issues. All
were repaired in `empirics/bid12.py` and registered in **rulebook §5.1
(calibration addendum, 2026-08-30)** before the final treated outputs were
derived:

1. Tender events with an unavailable header probe could stay confirmed, and
   route-B tender hits skipped the mandated header verification entirely
   (taking the first non-target `display_names` entry as bidder). Now every
   SC TO-T / SC TO-C event is header-verified; unavailable / truncated /
   unreadable headers → ambiguous; FILED BY cross-checked against all
   non-target `display_names` CIKs.
2. 8-K fetches had no truncation flag; truncated text could confirm. Now a
   document hitting its byte cap is ambiguous ("text-truncated", rulebook
   §5 row 8 as written).
3. The acquirer direction missed the rulebook's bare "acquire/acquired"
   cases (over-ambiguity, not false confirms). The bare form is implemented
   with the two guards registered in §5.1 item 1.
4. `lookup_treated` emitted BID12=0 for missing extraction caches; such rows
   now leave BID12 empty with `extraction_status=not-extracted` (rulebook
   §7), and the run metadata counts them.
5. (Smaller) header byte-cap truncation no longer degrades silently; a
   route-B query that cannot run for lack of a usable firm name is surfaced
   and counted instead of swallowed; the fixture-calibrated regex extras are
   now registered in the rulebook rather than living only in the code.

Verification: 35/35 synthetic checks (the 24 pre-existing synthetic checks

## 8. Coverage caveats (standing)

- **Route B recall** depends on EDGAR full-text search indexing (2001+) and on
  the target CIK appearing in the hit's `display_names`; name-only hits whose
  display names omit the target CIK are discarded and counted (logged in run
  metadata). Quantified in §7.
- **SC TO-C** (written communication) coverage depends on the bidder actually
  filing one; deals that go straight to SC TO-T have no TO-C.
- **8-K confirmation** is calibrated on 8 fixtures; the decision table's
  ambiguous branch (both directions, or no direction) is the safety valve and
  is audited in §6.
- **Extraction window** 2021-01-01 → 2026-12-31 covers TD+365 for TDs through
  2025-12-31; rows near the edges are flagged `window_coverage=partial`.
- **fact2_parsed.jsonl churn**: the file was re-parsed by another agent during
  the session; the pass re-deduplicates at start and records the input hash.
- Solver-style NA rows: none expected here, but firms whose submissions feed
  fails permanently are retried on the next run (transient failures are not
  cached); `extraction_status` in `bid12_treated.csv` marks any firm still
  missing.
