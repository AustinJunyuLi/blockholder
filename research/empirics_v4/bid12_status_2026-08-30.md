Filer's-own-bid rows: 79 overall; 65 among the 321 non-excluded BID12=1 (20%).

**Base rate vs the 18.1% anchor (SPEC §8.4): 321/3,325 = 9.7% — LOWER, the
red-flag condition. Investigation:**

1. *Ambiguous-never-forced convention.* The 181 empty-ambiguous
   non-excluded rows (coded empty, not 1) are driven almost entirely by
   ambiguous 8-Ks (178/181). Counting them all as bids gives a 15.1% upper
   bound — still below 18.1%.
2. *What the 178 rest on.* 768 ambiguous 8-Ks are "merger pattern fires, no
   direction cue" (row 6). A hand sample shows three populations: (a) SPAC
   de-SPAC 8-Ks where the firm is the acquirer vehicle — correctly not
   confirmed; (b) update/amendment 8-Ks whose original announcement was
   confirmed separately — harmless; (c) "party to a Merger Agreement by and
   among Parent, Merger Sub and the Company" phrasings with no explicit
   direction phrase (e.g. AERKOMM 2024-12-04) — a genuine direction-pattern
   gap, conservative. The audit should expect a meaningful minority of (c).
3. *Sample and era.* GS's 18.1% is completed acquisitions within 12 months in
   their 1994–2006 sample; ours is bids (incl. failed/withdrawn) within 365
   days, TDs 2021–2025 (spanning the 2022–23 deal drought), over a broader
   universe (microcaps, SPACs, foreign issuers). Direction of bias vs the
   anchor is ambiguous a priori; the 9.7–15.1% band is not alarming once (1)
   and (2) are accounted.
4. *Route-B recall* — no gaps detected (§5).

**Ambiguous events: 1,361** = 1,323 8-Ks (768 merger-only row 6; 550 with
direction/defence/instrument pattern mixes; 5 text-truncated at the 2 MB/1.5 MB
