# Literature card index — ticket 01

**Date: 2026-08-19.** These are the verified reading cards for the v4 repositioning effort:
one file per paper under `research/cards/`, each written to `_TEMPLATE.md` (question; model/data
and method; results with honesty labels PROVED / NUMERICAL / ESTIMATED / ASSERTED; institutional
facts; strengths and open flanks; scope boundary; implications for our position; page-cited
quotes; verification log).

**The reading rule.** Every paper was read cover to cover from full text — never an abstract,
never from memory — by a dedicated Opus reader agent (model verified from the agent transcripts:
every reader ran on claude-opus-5; the "Reader:" self-labels in some card headers were
corrected accordingly), and then re-read by a **separate adversarial verifier** who never
saw the reader's reasoning. The verifier's job was to refute: every quote was grepped against the
source text (`pdftotext -layout`, per-page where the page mattered, rendered page images where the
text layer drops Greek symbols), every honesty label re-checked against the printed proposition or
table, and every "they never do X" scope claim re-run as a full-text search. Verdicts are OK /
WRONG (fixed in place) / MISCITED (page or attribution swapped) / UNCHECKED (left in place, marked,
and listed in §3 below). Each card's header states which version was read and **which page
numbering the card uses** (printed journal page vs PDF page), because roughly a third of the set
was read from a working paper rather than the typeset article.

**Count: 42 cards in the table below** (11 competitor set, 15 theory antecedents, 14 empirics and
measurement, 2 surveys), plus `corum_levit_2019_wp2016_superseded.md` — the 2016 working paper of
Corum–Levit, kept only for the record and **superseded** by the published-JFE card; its formal
apparatus does not match the published article and must not be cited. `_institutional_sec_33_11253.md`
is an institutional card on the SEC release, not a paper.

**Corrections to our old notes — each of these was wrong before the reading.**

1. **Johnson–Swem contains no σ²T statement.** The window appears once, in background footnote 13. 5. **The published Corum–Levit has an explicit 13D threshold ᾱ** — "e.g., regulation 13D", "Empirically, ᾱ ∈ [5%, 10%]" (p. 10). The threshold margin is occupied, not free; the **window** is what is absent. *(`corum_levit_2019_jfe_published.md`)*

**Hazards — the traps a referee will set.**

20. **The hump is not ours.** Maug's **Proposition 7 (p. 83) already proves** initial shareholders' wealth is non-monotone in liquidity, maximised at an interior φ*; Edmans (2009) Prop. 3 proves an inverse-U in price efficiency; and **LMM (2024) prove their non-monotone-in-depth welfare result in full** (confirmed in the Internet Appendix). 21. **CCKV's liquidity result runs the other way.** Theorem 2(iii): as σ → ∞ manipulation does **not** vanish — more liquidity does not kill it, because the leader's incentive is her own endogenous terminal block (p. 24). There is no interior optimum. A generic "liquidity has a hump" claim will be met with this. *(`cetemen_cisternas_kolb_viswanathan_2026_jf.md` R14)*
22. **A higher threshold can *lower* the premium.** Corum–Levit (published), p. 8: a higher α means the activist "puts less weight on her private benefits … which harms her ability to bargain a higher takeover premium". With α* = ᾱ that is a published statement about the threshold margin with the opposite sign to a naive disclosure story. And on b ≤ Δ the threshold is inert entirely (p. 14). *(`corum_levit_2019_jfe_published.md` R16, R17)*
23. **Kyle & Vila (1991) — now read.** It disclaims disclosure in fn. 1 (p. 54), takes the premium as exogenous (p. 56), refuses welfare (p. 67), and has the raider's presence common knowledge with only the quantity camouflaged (one round); it already owns the sign-reversing liquidity effect on takeover probability, and its mixing equilibria already contain an endogenous pooled/revealed split — so our partition must be stated as *rule-keyed* (stake + date), not as "the market never learns". What is unoccupied is a deadline that is a **legal filing window with a partition attached**, not merely a random horizon. *(`collin_dufresne_fos_2016_ecta.md`)*
