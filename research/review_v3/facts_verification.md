- **Burkart-Lee-Voss 2024 current publication status**: only the local ECGI working-paper PDF was read; I did not search for a possible subsequent journal placement.
- No claim was left fully UNCHECKED without at least one primary-source attempt; the 3 UNCHECKED items above are all partial (title/venue/direction confirmed, only a specific sub-number or qualifier unverified).
