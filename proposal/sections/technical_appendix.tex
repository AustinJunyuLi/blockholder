\appendix
\section{Model Derivations}
\label{app:model-derivations}

\subsection{Accumulation cost}

At daily rate $r$, the dealer's excess cost is
\[
\int_0^r\lambda_Lu\,du=\frac{\lambda_L}{2}r^2.
\]
The activist minimises
$\sum_{t=1}^d(\lambda_L/2)r_t^2$ subject to
$\sum_{t=1}^dr_t=x$. Convexity and the symmetric constraint imply
$r_t=x/d$. Hence
\[
d\frac{\lambda_L}{2}\left(\frac{x}{d}\right)^2
=\frac{\lambda_L}{2}\frac{x^2}{d}.
\]
Differentiation gives equation~\eqref{eq:cost-derivs} and the degree-minus-one
homogeneity in $d$.

\subsection{Stake comparative static}

In the unconstrained region, the first-order condition is $b_x-C_x=0$.
Applying the implicit function theorem gives
\[
\frac{d\,\Delta x^\ast}{dd}
=-\frac{b_{xd}-C_{xd}}{b_{xx}-C_{xx}}.
\]
Strict concavity makes the denominator negative, so the derivative is positive
if and only if $b_{xd}>C_{xd}$. At the constrained corner,
$\Delta x_i^\ast=\phi\,\mathrm{ADV}_i d/S_{\mathrm{out},i}$, and direct
differentiation gives
\[
\frac{d\,\Delta x_i^\ast}{dd}
=\frac{\phi\,\mathrm{ADV}_i}{S_{\mathrm{out},i}}>0.
\]
