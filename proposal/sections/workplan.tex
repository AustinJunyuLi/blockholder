\section{The Next Eight Weeks: Gates and Cut Order}
\label{sec:workplan}

The schedule below is deliberately gate-first. The lens of Section~\ref{sec:model} exists in
outline form (R1--R6, stated in this document around the statutory trigger); the rule is that
no further model \emph{investment} --- full derivations, appendix machinery, calibration ---
and no price-dependent work is scheduled before the gates that decide whether those
investments pay. March 2027 is a
\emph{milestone talk} (first-stage facts, the exposure measure, the model skeleton, an anatomy
pilot), not a draft deadline; the full draft targets summer 2027.

\subsection*{Gate 0 (week 1): advisor ratification}
A two-page decision memo (compressed from the verified decision record) and a supervision
meeting. Every other gate is conditional on this one. The memo states plainly what is being
abandoned (the liquidity-hump headline as the job-market paper), what is being frozen (the
current 92-page draft, as a separate working paper after a bounded hygiene pass), and what is
being built.

\subsection*{Gate 1 (weeks 1--2): access and hygiene, in parallel}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{WRDS/CRSP go/no-go.} Account request submitted in week 1, calendar-booked. CRSP
gates the exposure denominator (ADV), all return-based outcomes, and the anatomy leg. If access
fails, the paper degrades along the published cut order below --- it does not stall.
\item \textbf{Bloomberg terminal inventory} (\texttt{lit/bloomberg\_checklist.md}): M\&A deal
fields, premia, export caps, academic terms of use. Decides the M\&A-outcomes route (Bloomberg
vs.\ hand-built EDGAR deal universe).
\item \textbf{Hygiene pass on the frozen draft} (bounded, $\le$2 weeks, parallel work): the
enumerated line-level corrections from the technical verification (cross-country threshold
claims removed; the $\kappa \to 1$ narrative corrected; welfare units rescaled; the two
misattributed citations fixed), then freeze.
\item \textbf{Gaussian-noise falsification} of the old model's hump (one week, closes the
question the pivot memo raised; result goes in the frozen draft's appendix, not this paper).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Gate 2 (weeks 2--4): the pre-registered EDGAR pass (price-free)}
Stake-at-filing distributions (untrimmed, split by corporate-action status), entry counts and
composition by the coarse required-trading-days proxy, Item~6 derivative-language rates,
13G-to-13D switching, each with a minimum detectable effect computed \emph{ex ante} from the
SEC's own tables. \textbf{Rule:} filing-delay compression and post-deadline pre-filing
accumulation are mechanical identities and cannot pass this gate. If at least one
non-mechanical margin moves, proceed to the full build; if none moves, the model shrinks to the
accounting lemma plus the corner condition, and the paper re-aims at the anatomy and the
well-measured policy null.

\subsection*{Weeks 5--8: conditional build}
The critical path is two items: the exposure measure on CRSP ADV (or the EDGAR turnover proxy
if CRSP is delayed) and the trigger-date parser fix with hand-verification against Item~5(c)
tables --- the two inputs the headline design cannot run without. The Item~4 purpose codebook
(3--5 weeks by the data map's own estimate) \emph{starts} in week 5 and is expected to finish
in weeks 8--10; the anatomy pilot on the hand-collected acquired-target subsample follows the
week-2 M\&A-route decision and may likewise overflow into weeks 9--12. The model section is
drafted to the specification in Section~\ref{sec:model} --- including the statutory-trigger
timing stated there --- \emph{only if} Gate 2 passed on a behavioral margin. Weeks 5--8
promise the critical path and the Gate-2 write-up; they do not promise the overflow items.

\subsection*{Published cut order (if the schedule slips)}
(1) merger-background chronologies stay out (no tooling exists in this repository --- revisit
only if tooling exists elsewhere); (2) the signaling block stays out (accounting lemma + corner
model ship without it); (3) the full deal-universe anatomy shrinks to the hand-collected
acquired-target subsample; (4) the 2010--2021 backfill is dropped (the SEC's published tables
anchor the pre-period); (5) if CRSP is still unresolved at week 4, the EDGAR-only configuration
(stake, timing, composition, derivatives) is the paper.

\subsection*{Scoop defense}
The novelty scan of 2026-08-02 found no realized-outcome study of the reform; the field is
attentive and the data are public. First-stage facts and the design go up as an SSRN working
paper as soon as the exposure design is locked (target: month 4--5), and the novelty scan and
the Celentano--Levine publication status are re-checked at every revision.
