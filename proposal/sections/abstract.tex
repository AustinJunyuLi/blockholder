% Candidate title 1 (evocative): Five Business Days: How the 2024 Schedule 13D Acceleration Changed Activist Stake Formation
% Candidate title 2 (descriptive): Disclosure Deadlines and Activist Ownership: A Realised Evaluation of the 2024 Schedule 13D Reform
% Candidate title 3 (conservative): The Effects of Accelerated Ownership Disclosure on Activist Campaigns and Target Shareholder Gains

In February 2024 the SEC shortened the Schedule 13D filing window from ten calendar days to five business days, while dropping a proposed rule covering cash-settled derivatives. We will provide the first realised evidence on this reform. We will construct a predetermined, target-level exposure measure --- the trading days required to add five percentage points of undisclosed ownership, which scales how much an activist can accumulate between crossing the disclosure threshold and the new deadline --- and test whether campaign entry and composition respond to the new deadline in proportion to exposure, and whether activists substitute equity accumulation with derivatives disclosed under Item 6, adjudicating the SEC's \$810 million annual foregone-value projection. A deadline-accumulation model with a liquidity-based price-impact microfoundation generates the exposure measure and organises the predictions; the deadline binds as a corner solution for a model-generated subset of targets. We will further decompose activist target gains into filing returns, post-filing run-ups, and bid markups, stated event-time-invariant, to establish where takeover value reaches target shareholders. All designs carry pre-registered minimum detectable effects and use the SEC's published tables as the pre-period anchor.
