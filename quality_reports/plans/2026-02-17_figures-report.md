- **Code changes:**
  - **model.py or solver.py**: New function `compute_welfare(k1, k0, kD, params) -> (W_min, W_B, W_bid, W_total)`

    - Sweep kappa, compute all four welfare components
    - Plot W_min(kappa), W_bid(kappa), W_total(kappa) on same axes
    - Mark kappa^dagger (peak of W_min) and kappa* (peak of W_total) with vertical lines
    - Legend with all curves labeled
    - Title: "Welfare Decomposition"
  - **This is the most complex new figure** because it requires computing blockholder and bidder surplus, not just minority gains. The existing `compute_minority_gains()` handles W_min but the other two components need new code.
