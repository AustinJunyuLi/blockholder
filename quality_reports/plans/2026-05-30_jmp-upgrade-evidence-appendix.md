## B. Numerical re-derivation results (against the paper's own price fixed point, baseline calibration, math.erf, no scipy)

- **Endpoint symmetry — EXACT.** $D{=}0$ conditional law of $\pi$ is the identical two-point distribution at $\kappa\to0$ and $\kappa\to1$: $\{\pi=0\ \text{w.p. }0.3125,\ \pi=\bar\pi=0.4545\ \text{w.p. }0.6875\}$; $\mathbb{E}[\pi]=0.3125$, $\mathrm{Var}=0.044389$, $P(D{=}0)=0.80$, all equal to $\sim10^{-10}$. Hence $\Delta^{\min}(0)=\Delta^{\min}(1)$.
- **$\mathrm{Var}[\pi\mid D{=}0]$ — robustly U-shaped** across six weight configs (argmin $\kappa\approx0.48$–$0.58$); baseline $V_0=0.04429$, $V_{\min}=0.01378$, $V_1=0.04418$ (endpoints equal to $\sim10^{-4}$).
- **Concavity at baseline:** both $g(\pi)=\bar m(\pi)p(\pi)$ ($\max g''=-0.058$) and the paper's actual integrand $h(\pi)=\pi p(\pi)$ ($\max h''=-0.415$) are concave; $(C^*)$ margin $-0.637$. Full $\Delta^{\min}$ is a genuine HUMP: interior max $\kappa\approx0.55$, equal endpoints, amplitude $\approx1.87\%$ of level.
- **$(C^*)$ is non-vacuous and correctly oriented (hump↔trough flips):**
  - baseline: margin $-0.637$, $g''=-0.058$ → **HUMP**, amplitude $1.87\%$.
  - $\sigma_\xi=0.25$: margin $-0.180$ → **HUMP**, amplitude $3.30\%$.
  - $\sigma_\xi=0.10$: margin $+1.18$, $g''>0$ → **TROUGH**.
  - $\bar S=0.60$: margin $+2.58$ → **TROUGH**.
- **Caveat:** baseline hump amplitude ($\sim1.9\%$) sits near solver `TOL_RESIDUAL = 5e-3` (params.py:29); sweep uses single warm-start `compute_series_over_kappa`, not multi-start `solve_valid`.

**Net validity verdict (headline lens):** memo's fix is *substantially correct at baseline* but not yet referee-proof. Remaining flaws: (i) $(C^*)$ stated for $g$, not the actual integrand $h=\pi p$; (ii) GE cutoff-shift channel is a genuine first-order unsigned term (envelope theorem cannot zero it — $\Delta^{\min}$ is not the blockholder's objective); (iii) $(C^*)$ must be a stated hypothesis (hump is not unconditional); (iv) endpoint symmetry removes the directional policy message → reframe around interior optimum $\kappa^\dagger$ tied to threshold; (v) variance U-shape and single-peakedness still need analytic (not numerical) proof.
