### Three defects in the draft `estimate_did.py` scaffold, fixed

1. **`logilliq_raw` does not exist** in `h1_sample.csv`. The draft renamed it to
   `logilliq` and would have raised on the first real run. The matching variable
   is `logilliq` (the raw log-Amihud); `liq` is its quarter-standardised
   transform and is not a matching variable.
2. **No-replacement was global.** SPEC §8.2 allows a control to serve treated
   firms in different quarters and clusters on the match group precisely for
   that. A global used-set against a 3,600-firm pool would have degraded the 3:1
   ratio through an implementation choice and reported the degradation as a
   pool shortfall. Now keyed on (PERMNO, quarter).
3. **`match_group` was the treated PERMNO.** The treated sample is one row per
   (firm, TD); a firm with two triggers collided its own match groups and shared
   controls between them. Now the treated accession.

### Two things written down rather than left implicit

- **Quarter-exactness is vacuous by construction.** SPEC §8.2 requires an exact
  match on the calendar quarter of TD *and* gives each control the treated
  firm's TD as its pseudo-trigger date, so a control has no quarter of its own
  to disagree with. The estimator asserts the invariant instead of filtering on
  it. (The handoff asked for this note.)
- **`λ·Post` is not identified alongside the match fixed effects** — Post is
  constant within a match group under pseudo-TD inheritance. Reported in the
  estimator's output, not worked around. Match FE are absorbed by FWL demeaning
  so the 9,999-draw wild bootstrap stays tractable; the equivalence to the
  explicit-dummy design is checked numerically on the estimation sample and
  emitted as `fwl_check`.

## 2. Control-pool finding — CORRECTED

**The first version of this section was wrong, and the correction is the
session's main finding.** It is left visible rather than rewritten.

*What it said.* The stage-M smoke run reported 1,122 of 3,464 CIK-linked control
PERMNOs with a blank SIC in EDGAR. I read them as closed-end funds and trusts,
and argued that excluding them was the exact-SIC match doing the universe
screen's job. Commit `7d46c4c` and a status message to Austin carry that claim.

*What is true.* CRSP classifies the same 1,122 as `IssuerType` CORP or REIT with
`SecuritySubType` COM — ordinary corporations. They resembled one fund because
the link map pointed them at one fund. `norm_ticker` turned a missing CRSP
ticker into the string `"NAN"`, which is the real NYSE ticker of Nuveen New York
Quality Municipal Income Fund, so **all 1,607 delisted PERMNOs were mapped to
CIK 1074769**. Full account, numbers and repair in
`research/empirics_v4/link_map_nan_ticker_2026-08-30.md`; fix and 19 checks in
`empirics/link_cik_cusip.py` and `empirics/test_link_cik_cusip.py`.

*What I should have done sooner.* The comfortable reading arrived first and I
reported it before testing it. The test that broke it — comparing EDGAR's
classification against CRSP's for the same PERMNOs — cost one query. A finding
that conveniently excuses a data loss deserves that query before it is written
down, not after.

*The consequence, for Austin.* Blank SIC collapses from 1,122 to 2. In its place
sits a real problem: no delisted control firm can be coded for BID12 at all, so
the control group is now conditioned on survival while the treated side keeps
its acquired firms. The control bid rate biases down and γ biases up. Three
options are laid out in the dated note; none is taken here.
