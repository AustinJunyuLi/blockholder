### Three defects in the draft `estimate_did.py` scaffold, fixed

1. **`logilliq_raw` does not exist** in `h1_sample.csv`. The draft renamed it to
   `logilliq` and would have raised on the first real run. The matching variable
   is `logilliq` (the raw log-Amihud); `liq` is its quarter-standardised
   transform and is not a matching variable.
2. **No-replacement was global.** SPEC §8.2 allows a control to serve treated
   firms in different quarters and clusters on the match group precisely for
   that. A global used-set against a 3,600-firm pool would have degraded the 3:1
   ratio through an implementation choice and reported the degradation as a
   pool shortfall. Now keyed on (PERMNO, quarter).
3. **`match_group` was the treated PERMNO.** The treated sample is one row per
   (firm, TD); a firm with two triggers collided its own match groups and shared
   controls between them. Now the treated accession.

### Two things written down rather than left implicit

- **Quarter-exactness is vacuous by construction.** SPEC §8.2 requires an exact
  match on the calendar quarter of TD *and* gives each control the treated
  firm's TD as its pseudo-trigger date, so a control has no quarter of its own
  to disagree with. The estimator asserts the invariant instead of filtering on
  it. (The handoff asked for this note.)
- **`λ·Post` is not identified alongside the match fixed effects** — Post is
  constant within a match group under pseudo-TD inheritance. Reported in the
  estimator's output, not worked around. Match FE are absorbed by FWL demeaning
  so the 9,999-draw wild bootstrap stays tractable; the equivalence to the
  explicit-dummy design is checked numerically on the estimation sample and
  emitted as `fwl_check`.
