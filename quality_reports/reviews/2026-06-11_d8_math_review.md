Additional independent probes run at sub-grid, out-of-grid, and counterexample points (details below).

## Verdict: SOUND-WITH-ISSUES

The quantitative core is solid and reproduces: the inversion-free bound chain (Neumann + Hoelder) in Lemma d8-cutoff is correct; the certificates replicate exactly; the inclusion inversion-free-region within exact-region is a logical necessity, not an accident; and the sigma_xi = 0.60 counterexample is genuine (reproduced 33/33 solves, trough robust to multistart at the minimizer, spread 5.7e-9). However, the record contains one real overclaim in the theorem statement (the "changes sign exactly once" clause does not follow from R1-R2 inside the epsilon-ball), one wrong displayed equation (the inference term of eq. d8-weights is missing a factor (m_tilde - m_0) and evaluates with the wrong sign at representative points), and one remark whose "verified at the baseline" sign claims are both false against the actual equilibrium cells. None of these invalidates the certified region or the counterexample, because the numerics bypass the flawed displays (weights by finite differences; certification of the realized E and B profiles directly), but the prose claims more than the proofs deliver in three specific places.

## (a) Theorem statement: what is claimed, where, and how quantified

**What is claimed.** Theorem `thm:d8-region` is a statement about the parameter kappa (noise-trading intensity) on a sub-interval [kappa_lo, kappa_hi], at one fixed calibration. "Dominance" means: (R1) the computable upper bound Bbar(kappa) on the GE channel |B(kappa)| is pointwise below |E'(kappa)| (channel A) outside an epsilon-ball around the channel-A peak kappa_A-dagger, and (R2) the integral of Bbar over the ball is below an E-gap margin. Conclusion: Delta_min strictly increasing left of the ball, strictly decreasing right of it, maximum interior to the ball, "in particular dDelta_min/dkappa changes sign exactly once" and Hypothesis hyp:d1-cutoffshift "holds as a theorem" on the interval. sigma_xi enters only through the counterexample (different calibration cells, sigma_xi = 0.60 vs baseline 0.40); the certified regions [0.30, 0.85] (exact IFT bound) and [0.35, 0.825] (inversion-free bound) are kappa-intervals at the baseline calibration only. The manuscript (draft_v2.tex lines 952-961, 2839) scopes this correctly as "at the baseline."

**Quantification problems.**
1. The conditional clause "Maintain ... (C*) on [kappa_lo, kappa_hi], **so that** channel (A) ... is single-peaked with interior maximizer kappa_A-dagger" (D8 tex line 169) asserts an implication that is not proven anywhere. Lemma lem:d1-jensen is a *fixed-cutoff* statement, and even at fixed cutoffs its proof delivers only "interior values strictly above the equal endpoints" (an interior global max), not rise-then-fall single-peakedness. The path object E(kappa) = integral of dPhi/dkappa evaluated along the *moving* cutoff path needs single-crossing of kappa |-> kappa_dagger(k(kappa)) - kappa, which is an extra regularity. The project's own earlier record is explicit about exactly this: `D1_prop6_condition_Cstarstar.tex` header, item (P2): "**NOT single-peakedness; the word 'peak' is withheld here**", with unimodality demoted to Numerical Regularity (NR1). D8's premise silently re-asserts the withheld implication. Empirically it is fine — my probes confirm channel A's finite-difference profile crosses zero exactly once on the baseline grid and once on the sigma_xi=0.60, S_bar=1.64 grid — but E-single-peakedness should be an explicit, script-verified hypothesis (an "R0"), not a consequence attributed to (C*).
2. Corollary cor:d8-baseline says the hump "is a theorem conditional only on the maintained Assumptions A1-A7 and (C*)". It is additionally conditional on: the unproven (C*)-to-single-peak step above, grid-level certification (23 points, spacing 0.025, no interval arithmetic), and the FD/solver fidelity assumptions of item (e).

## (b) Proof logic, step by step

- **Lemma lem:d8-pricing (pricing IFT).** The feedback margin ell(P,u) = delta[p + (dp/dP)(P + mbar - Vhat)] is the correct derivative of the fixed-point RHS (re-derived; confirmed). The quotient formula is correct and the script verifies it to 7.2e-11 (3 generic points). **Gap:** the proof's parenthetical claims the body's sufficient condition delta/sigma_xi < 1/phi(0) "gives a uniform ell_bar < 1". It does not by itself: ell < 1 needs delta*p + delta*|p'|*|P + mbar - Vhat| < 1, which requires a bound on the takeover-vs-standalone value gap, never stated. At the baseline delta*sup|dp/dP| = 0.947, so the crude bound exceeds 1 whenever the gap exceeds ~0.05 with the unfavorable sign. Numerically harmless here — at sampled equilibrium-style cells the gap is positive (so the p' term *reduces* ell) and ell ranges 0.03-0.43 — but the analytic claim is incomplete.
- **Lemma lem:d8-cutoff (inversion-free bound).** Fully correct. D_kF = I - D_kT with ||D_kT||_inf <= L < 1 gives invertibility with Neumann bound 1/(1-L); dk/dkappa = (I - D_kT)^{-1} d_kappa T (signs check out: d_kappa F = -d_kappa T); |B| <= (sum_i W_i)*||dk/dkappa||_inf is the l1-linfty Hoelder pairing. The claim that everything is computable from one solve plus one finite-difference column is accurate (verified against the script).
- **Lemma lem:d8-weights.** The decomposition's *existence/continuity* content is fine, but the displayed inference term (eq. d8-weights, tex line 145) is **algebraically wrong**: differentiating mbar(pi)*p(mbar(pi), P) in k_i gives (m_tilde - m_0)*[p + mbar*dp/dmbar]*dpi/dk_i, whereas the display has [(m_tilde - m_0)*p + mbar*dp/dmbar]*dpi/dk_i — the factor (m_tilde - m_0) = 0.18 is missing on the second piece. Direct FD check at a representative cell (pi = 0.5, P = 0.95): true derivative +0.08452 (matches the corrected bracket to 1e-8); the displayed bracket gives **-0.06032** — wrong magnitude *and sign*. Downstream damage is nil only because (i) the script computes W_i by central finite differences of Phi, never using the display, and (ii) Remark rem:d8-signs(i)'s sign criterion "positive iff p > mbar*phi(t)/sigma_xi" happens to match the *corrected* algebra, not the printed one.
- **Theorem thm:d8-region, proof.**
  - Off-ball monotonicity from (R1): correct (|B| <= Bbar < |E'| forces sign(Delta') = sign(E') there; R1 with Bbar >= 0 also implicitly forces E' != 0 off the ball, which is fine as a hypothesis).
  - Maximizer in the closed ball: correct (outer segments monotone).
  - Maximizer *interior* to the ball: the written argument routes this through (R2) ("the maximum over the ball strictly exceeds both segment endpoints"), which does not exclude a maximizer sitting exactly at a ball edge. The conclusion is nevertheless recoverable without (R2): R1 holds *at* the edge points kappa_A-dagger +/- epsilon (they lie in the off-ball set as defined), so Delta'(left edge) > 0 and Delta'(right edge) < 0, and with Delta' continuous (C^1 from lem:d8-cutoff) the edges cannot be maximizers. Proof gap, conclusion fine.
  - **"dDelta_min/dkappa changes sign exactly once on [kappa_lo, kappa_hi]" (tex line 174): NOT established, and not true under the stated hypotheses.** The proof only rules out sign changes *outside* the ball ("were there two interior sign changes of the derivative outside the ball..."). Inside the ball, R1 is silent and R2 is only an integral bound: take E' single-peaked with E'(peak) = 0 and B a small continuous oscillation inside the ball with |B| <= Bbar, pointwise amplitude exceeding |E'| near the peak but tiny integral — R1 and R2 hold, yet Delta' has three sign changes. The true, provable conclusion is: exactly one sign change *outside* the ball, an odd number of sign changes all confined to the ball, and the global max interior to the ball ("single-peaked up to epsilon-localization", which the proof's own final parenthetical half-concedes). Since the exactly-once form is precisely the form in which Hypothesis hyp:d1-cutoffshift is stated, the clause "the conclusion of Hypothesis hyp:d1-cutoffshift holds ... as a theorem" is an overclaim; only an epsilon-weakened version is discharged. The manuscript's flat "certifies single-peakedness as a theorem on the interior region" (draft_v2.tex lines 956-957, and the fig:ge-decomposition caption) inherits this without even the epsilon caveat.
  - Side observations: (R2) is logically redundant for the *stated* conclusions (given strict R1 at the ball edges and C^1 regularity) while insufficient for the overclaimed final clause — the hypothesis set and conclusion set are mismatched in both directions. The amplitude A* appears in the statement but is never used in R1, R2, or the conclusions (vestigial). The premise notation "E(kappa) = Phi(k(kappa_bar_0), kappa)-type fixed-cutoff paths" uses an undefined symbol kappa_bar_0 and mischaracterizes E, which is the path integral of the frozen-cutoff kappa-derivative along the *moving* path (this is what the script computes).
- **Computer-assisted steps clearly labeled?** Mostly yes — the epistemic ledger and Corollary cor:d8-baseline explicitly name the script and JSON artifact; this is good practice. Two mislabels: "L <= 0.836 < 1 along the **entire path**" (it is a 23-point grid sample; no interval arithmetic or Lipschitz bridging), and Remark rem:d8-signs presents two sign claims as "verified at the baseline" that the script never checks and that are in fact false as stated (see (f) and issue D8-3).

## (c) The counterexample

**Does it violate the property outside the region?** Yes, and it reproduces. At sigma_xi = 0.60, S_bar in {1.44, 1.54, 1.64} (kappa grid [0.10, 0.90], 33 points): interior minimizers at kappa = 0.275, 0.35, 0.475 with depths 5.4e-4, 1.4e-3, 2.8e-3 (all >= 5x the 1e-4 trough gate, ~4-6 orders above solver noise); channel A has an interior max above the endpoints in every cell; int|B| exceeds the channel-A amplitude in every cell (0.0207 vs 0.0069 at S_bar = 1.64, matching the tex's "0.021 vs 0.0069"). S_bar = 1.24, 1.34 are correctly *not* claimed (1.34's interior dip is 5.9e-5, below the gate). My independent probes strengthen this: (i) channel A's FD profile crosses zero exactly once at S_bar = 1.64 (so the rise-then-fall premise needed by step (iii) holds in the data, not just interior-max — see gap below); (ii) the cutoff path is continuous near the trough (max adjacent-kappa cutoff jump 0.084 occurs at the kappa = 0.10 grid edge, far from the trough); (iii) multistart at the trough kappa = 0.475 (cold start + warm from both neighbors) returns one fixed point, Delta_min spread 5.7e-9 — **the trough is not a branch-selection artifact** (this robustness check is mine; it is not in the committed artifact).

**Logic gaps (none fatal).**
1. Step (iii)'s inference "a trough ... impossible unless |B| > |E'| on a set of positive measure" is valid only if channel A is genuinely single-peaked (rise-then-fall); if E merely has an interior max but dips below its endpoint values elsewhere, a Delta-trough is compatible with |B| <= |E'|. The script's `chanA_singlepeaked` flag actually tests only max(E_path) > endpoint values (interior max), a strictly weaker property than its name and than what (iii) uses. My probe closes this empirically for the headline cell (one sign crossing), but the certificate as coded is weaker than the property cited.
2. Step (i) cites Remark rem:d5-vacuous, which proves concavity of g(pi) = mbar(pi)*p(pi), to invoke Lemma lem:d1-jensen, whose condition (C*) is the chord condition on h(pi) = pi*p(pi) — a different function that the D1 record itself insists is the right one ("the memo's g-based condition certifies the wrong function"). The missing one-line bridge (a(pi) < 0 implies p' < 0 and p'' < 0, hence h'' = 2p' + pi*p'' < 0, so both g and h are concave on the family) should be stated; note also that a(pi) < 0 is derived in D5 under the surrogate P(pi) = mbar(pi), not the equilibrium prices (see (f)).
3. The final sentence "at the sigma_xi = 0.60 cells the certified inequalities (R1)-(R2) **correctly fail**" is asserted but never computed: the counterexample routine evaluates neither Bbar, nor L, nor R1/R2 at those cells. As a modus tollens it additionally requires the *other* theorem hypotheses (quantified contraction L < 1, channel-A single-peakedness) to hold at those cells, which is also unverified — if L >= 1 there, the theorem simply would not apply and nothing about R1-R2 follows.
4. Proof text says the cells are re-solved "at tightened gates" — the script uses the default `TOL_RESIDUAL = 5e-3` via `solve_valid` (no tightening). And "on the reference (non-JIT) path" is true in this environment only because numba is not installed in `.venv`; `numerical/solver.py` auto-dispatches to the accel path when numba is importable, so the script does not *enforce* the claim.

**Does it contradict anything else in the manuscript?** No. It is consistent with the conditional framing of Proposition prop:nonmonotone, with Hypothesis hyp:d1-cutoffshift being explicitly fallible, and with Remark rem:d5-conditional. One unexplained cross-record discrepancy: rem:d5-conditional and the D1 record report "4/20 trough cells (all at sigma_xi = 0.60)" while D8 certifies 3 of the 5 sigma_xi = 0.60 cells (S_bar = 1.34 falls below D8's depth gate; presumably a grid/threshold difference, but no record reconciles 4 vs 3).

## (d) Inversion-free [0.35, 0.825] vs exact [0.30, 0.85]

**What differs.** Three bounds are computed per grid point: exact B_ift = <grad Phi, (I - D_kT)^{-1} d_kappa T> (requires solving the linear system — "inversion" of the cutoff-layer Jacobian); sharp = (sum W)*||dk/dkappa||_inf; loose/inversion-free Bbar = (sum W)*||d_kappa T||_inf / (1 - L) (needs only the contraction modulus, no solve of the linear system). The theorem's operative bound (eq. d8-Bbar) is the loose one.

**Is inversion-free within exact logically necessary?** Yes. Pointwise |B_ift| <= (sum W)*||dk/dkappa||_inf (Hoelder) <= (sum W)*||d_kappa T||_inf/(1 - L) (Neumann), so any kappa that passes R1 under the loose bound passes under the exact bound, and the contiguous-interval construction preserves inclusion. Verified numerically at every grid point (e.g. kappa = 0.85: exact 8.2e-3 <= sharp 1.6e-2 <= loose 5.1e-2). The inclusion [0.35, 0.825] within [0.30, 0.85] is strict because the loose bound fails R1 at 0.30 (-19% margin), 0.325 (-2.6%), and 0.85 (-5.2%).

**Consistency with the trough at sigma_xi = 0.60.** No tension: both certified intervals are kappa-intervals at the *baseline* calibration (sigma_xi = 0.40); the troughs live in different calibration cells (sigma_xi = 0.60). The trough minimizers (0.275-0.475) overlapping the baseline kappa-region numerically is irrelevant — the certificates are calibration-specific, and the manuscript states this correctly. The channel-A peak at kappa = 0.60 (baseline) is consistent with both intervals containing it, and with the manuscript's Delta-peak kappa-dagger ~ 0.59 (grid values: Dmin(0.575) = 0.0775415 vs Dmin(0.60) = 0.0775502).

**Asymmetry worth flagging:** the exact interval [0.30, 0.85] is **grid-limited, not failure-limited** — it coincides exactly with the tested grid's span. My out-of-grid probes show exact-bound R1 also passes at kappa = 0.25, 0.275, 0.875, and 0.90 (e.g. 0.25: |B_ift| = 8.7e-3 vs |E'| = 3.5e-2). So the exact region is conservative; by contrast the inversion-free interval's endpoints are genuinely bracketed by failures (0.325 fails, 0.35 passes). The record nowhere notes this asymmetry.

## (e) Check-script adequacy

**What it certifies vs samples.** Everything is *sampled*: 23 grid points (spacing 0.025) on the baseline path, 33 on each counterexample cell. No interval arithmetic, no Lipschitz/modulus-of-continuity bridging between nodes, so R1, L < 1, and the certified intervals are strictly grid-point statements; the tex's "along the entire path" and interval phrasing extrapolate by an implicit continuity appeal.

**Where a grid could miss a violation.**
- *Exact bound:* margins are very wide off the ball (worst off-ball point kappa = 0.525: |B_ift| = 4.0e-4 vs |E'| = 9.0e-3, ~96% margin), so a sub-grid R1_exact violation would require pathological wiggle; low risk.
- *Inversion-free bound:* margins at the certified boundary are thin — 2.9% at kappa = 0.35 and 6.1% at 0.825. My sub-grid probes at 0.34, 0.355, 0.3625, 0.81, 0.8375 all pass (0.34 passes with a 1.0% margin), locating the true crossover in (0.325, 0.34); no sub-grid violation found inside [0.35, 0.825], but the certificate has no formal protection there.
- *Contraction modulus:* L is a central-FD estimate (h = 1e-4) of ||D_kT||_inf at the solved point only; the theorem needs a neighborhood-uniform L. Max sampled L = 0.836 leaves a 0.164 margin; un-bridged between nodes.
- *R2 for the sub-interval:* the script computes the margin once with the full-grid endpoints E(0.30), E(0.85) and reuses it for all three bounds. For the loose-bound sub-interval [0.35, 0.825] the proper margin uses E(0.35) = 1.46e-3 and E(0.825) = 1.11e-4, giving 3.28e-3 instead of 4.75e-3. The loose ball integral 3.75e-4 still passes by 8.7x, so the conclusion stands; the methodology is nevertheless wrong for sub-intervals.
- *Root-finder fallback:* `_root()` silently returns argmin|g| when the 121-point scan finds no sign change — a potential undetected corruption of T-derivative columns. Never flagged or counted. Mitigated by the strong residual-vs-IFT agreement (max 5.0e-6 against tolerance max(0.25*scale, 5e-3)) on the baseline path, which would likely have caught it there.
- *FD-of-solver noise:* total derivative via warm-started re-solves at h_k = 2e-3 under the residual gate 5e-3; the B_resid vs B_ift cross-check (5e-6) indicates the realized solver noise is far below the gate; adequate.

**Untested claims (asserted in the tex, absent from the script):** (1) Remark rem:d8-signs(i) and (ii) entirely — and both fail when checked (see (f)); (2) "R1-R2 correctly fail at the sigma_xi = 0.60 cells" — never evaluated, nor is L < 1 there; (3) pricing-layer quotient "cell by cell" — the script checks 3 generic pi values at E[v] = mu, not the equilibrium's reached cells; (4) channel-A single-*peakedness* (only interior-max is coded; sign-crossing count not recorded); (5) counterexample branch uniqueness (no multistart in the script; my probe supplies it for the S_bar = 1.64 trough); (6) any sub-grid or out-of-grid behavior.

## (f) Hidden assumptions

1. **Collapsed-Hold boundary regime.** All 23 baseline rows have k0 = k1 (collapsed); the lemma/theorem are stated for the 3-cutoff system, but the IFT is actually applied (in the script) to the reduced (k1, kD) system on the collapsed manifold. This is the right object, but the tex record never mentions the reduction or the implicit corner-stability assumption (that small kappa- and k-perturbations keep the equilibrium collapsed); only the script docstring does. The residual-vs-IFT agreement (5e-6) is good indirect evidence of consistency.
2. **The D5 surrogate vs equilibrium prices.** Remark rem:d8-signs(i) claims "At the baseline path t < 0 on all reached cells (Remark rem:d5-anegative)". rem:d5-anegative derives a(pi) < 0 under the D5 shortcut P(pi) = mbar(pi); the actual equilibrium prices are 0.84-1.90, and direct computation at the baseline kappa = 0.5 equilibrium gives **t > 0 on 5 of 7 reached cells** (t = +2.22 on the disclosed branch, bid probability 1.3%). Moreover the deterrence-moderation condition p > mbar*phi(t)/sigma_xi *fails* on the four pi = 1 cells (-0.0105, -0.0062) — benign only because dpi/dk_i = 0 on those cells at the collapsed baseline, so the inference term carries no weight there; it holds on the two operative cells (-1,0): +0.38 and (0,0): +0.12. Remark rem:d8-signs(ii)'s claimed *negative* sign of the e_D boundary term is also wrong at kappa = 0.5: per unit mass, the Quiet-side value is ~0.074 vs the disclosed-side m_tilde*p_1 ~ 0.0037, so the stated difference is decisively **positive** (disclosure deters bids through the price run-up, so moving mass from Public to Quiet *raises* Sum mu*mbar*p). The remark is explicitly non-load-bearing for the theorem ("not needed for Theorem thm:d8-region"), but both of its "verified at the baseline" claims are false and unverified by the script.
3. **Solver convergence / NA handling.** Points failing the 5e-3 residual gate are silently dropped (none were: 23/23 and 33/33 valid); a dropped interior point would silently shrink the certified set without warning. The "tightened gates" claim in prop:d8-counter's proof is inaccurate (default gate used).
4. **Continuity arguments used implicitly:** continuity of E', Bbar, L between grid nodes (for the interval reading); continuity of Delta' inside the ball (legitimate, from the C^1 lemmas); convergence of the warm-started FD re-solves to the same branch.
5. **Equilibrium uniqueness** (A6 contraction) remains assumed, as the record itself states; interiority of cutoffs holds comfortably on the path (kD in [2.25, 2.47], k1 in [0.69, 0.83], well inside mu +/- 6 sigma_s).
