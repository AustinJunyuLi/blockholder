# Referee report

## What I would say first

I would not recommend acceptance in the current form. The status labels and numerical trail are unusually careful, and the Blackwell garbling result is the strongest part of the paper. But the legal clock does not map into the calibrated clock, several formal claims are unlabelled, and the appendix has proof and boundary defects. The current economic result is a fixed-policy information experiment, not yet a result about equilibrium behavior or the effect of the disclosure reform.

## Is this the strongest framing?

No. The title and opening invite a rule-design result about observable blockholders who are caught. The strongest defensible framing for the current results is a narrower note on information accounting in a fixed-policy order-flow model, centered on the garbling lemma. The paper could sustain its present framing only after it gives the legal clock a coherent unit, makes the public information set institutionally credible, and either supplies an equilibrium foundation or states much more plainly that the cutoff assignment is exogenous. The "who gets caught" language is also too literal because the caught leg includes the re-pricing of survivors and is not, in general, a statistic of the newly flagged histories.

## Blocking items

1. **The statement of the US filing obligation is false as written.** The paper says that a holder who "crosses 5 percent" must file Schedule 13D. The statutory trigger is beneficial ownership of more than 5 percent, and eligible passive and institutional holders can report on Schedule 13G. The current sentence describes neither the threshold nor the filing population correctly. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 2-3, Sections 1 and 2.1; `paper.tex:78-82` and `paper.tex:153-156`.

2. **The legal clock and the numerical clock use different units.** The institution changes from ten calendar days to five business days. The model defines the filing date as `c + T` in trading rounds, but the main clock record compares `T=10` with `T=5` and discusses that comparison next to the legal reform. Ten calendar days is not ten trading rounds. The choice also forces `T=10=H` into a corner with a nearly empty flagged cell. The numerical statements are valid for the recorded model nodes, but they are not a calibrated comparison of the old and new US clocks. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 3, 5, and 13, Sections 2.1, 3.2, and 5; `paper.tex:153-161`, `paper.tex:254-271`, and `paper.tex:705-732`; `numerical_v4/checks/t2_t1_check.json`, check `t1_block4_window_margin`.

3. **The literature section misnames the formal outcome.** It calls the object "the liquidity sensitivity of an expected takeover premium." The formal object is only the engagement-related component, `Delta^act = Delta_m E[pi p]`. It omits the baseline premium term `m_0 E[p]`, which can move with liquidity when entry moves. No result shows that the reported factorisation or signs extend to the total takeover premium or shareholder welfare invoked in the surrounding literature discussion. Locations: paper.pdf p. 4, Sections 2.2-2.3, and p. 7, Section 3.5; `paper.tex:180-193` and `paper.tex:391-397`.

4. **Several shipped analytic and numerical claims have no honesty label.** The claim that `m_0 >= 0` gives a unique continuous pricing root is unlabelled, and its proof is not in the delivered appendix. The fixed-policy claim that a shorter clock weakly lowers stake at filing is also unlabelled and is later used to read the estimates; calling it a timing "property" does not change its status as an analytic result. Section 5 then states, without a label, that every selected Voice path finishes building by filing and that `B^F` equals the target, followed by the numerical factorisation residual and flat-weight check. The `B^F` equality is not reported in an allowed numerical result record. Locations: paper.pdf p. 5, Section 3.3; p. 7, Section 3.5; pp. 11-12, Section 5; and p. 15, Section 6.2; `paper.tex:285-290`, `paper.tex:409-414`, `paper.tex:643-657`, and `paper.tex:829-834`.

5. **The pooled-experiment proofs use missing independence hypotheses.** The appendix setup gives the one-round ternary probabilities but does not assume that noise is independent across rounds or independent of type. Lemma 5(a) and its proof invoke both facts, and Lemmas 6-7 need them for the deletion kernel and the product-Bernoulli representation. The main model prose states independence, but the appendix presents formal statements and full hypothesis lists without importing it. Add the iid and type-independence clauses to the theorem hypotheses or explicitly incorporate the paper-model assumptions. Locations: appendix.pdf pp. 13-18, Section 2; `proofs/02_garbling.tex:28-56`, `proofs/02_garbling.tex:91-125`, `proofs/02_garbling.tex:236-242`, and `proofs/02_garbling.tex:283-310`.

6. **The pooled-experiment block is undefined at a null pooled cell, while the paper also overstates the endpoint cells.** The appendix defines `rho_P = rho(. | D=0)` without requiring `Omega<1`. Lemma 5(c) claims its posterior formula for every cell event, and Lemma 6 concludes that the pooled posterior has mean `rho_P`, again without positive pooled mass. These objects are not determined when `Omega=1`, as the appendix itself correctly explains for `M_P`. Separately, paper Lemma 1 says an empty-weight cell is a null set "rather than an empty one"; the hypotheses allow the event to be literally empty. Locations: paper.pdf p. 8, Section 4.1; `paper.tex:429-437`; appendix.pdf pp. 13-15, Section 2; `proofs/02_garbling.tex:49-56`, `proofs/02_garbling.tex:83-125`, and `proofs/02_garbling.tex:295-305`.

7. **The appendix artifact does not carry a consistent honesty status.** Its formal lemmas, propositions, theorems, and corollary have no printed `PROVED` labels. More directly, the assembled source still says `Label at write time: CONJECTURE` for the who-gets-caught result, while the paper labels the same result `PROVED`. A conjecture marker cannot coexist with the delivered proved claim. Locations: appendix.pdf pp. 1-24; `appendix.tex:44-61`; `proofs/03_caught.tex:14`; paper.pdf pp. 10-11, Section 4.4; `paper.tex:604-636`.

8. **The appendix's final reading of the clock criterion is false.** It says that a shorter clock lowers sensitivity when what it removes is what the market was reading most from order flow. The proved attenuation criterion is instead that signed `s_B` lies between 0 and `(2/phi)s_A`. For example, with `phi=1/2`, `s_A=1`, and `s_B=1/2`, attenuation holds even though the caught leg is less sensitive than the pool. If `s_B` is too large, attenuation fails even though it is the most sensitive leg. The informal sentence drops both parts of the exact criterion. Locations: appendix.pdf p. 24, Section 3.1; `proofs/03_caught.tex:357-358`.

9. **The `ESTIMATED` label does not meet the paper's stated label rule.** The label covers annual and period means, quantiles, and clock medians. None has a standard error. Some differences and shares have percentile-bootstrap intervals, but the result file reports no standard errors, and many displayed estimates have neither an interval nor a standard error. Either supply the required uncertainty for the estimates or narrow the labelled result to objects that satisfy the rule. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 14-16, Section 6; `paper.tex:761-781` and `paper.tex:786-870`; `empirics/output/e1_estimate.json`.

10. **Figure 1 identifies finite differences as analytic derivatives.** The caption says the plotted sensitivities are slopes over consecutive grid nodes, then labels them `S = |partial_kappa Delta^act|` and `S_P = |partial_kappa M_P|`. A secant over a 0.01 interval is not the point derivative defined in Section 3.5, and no approximation error is reported. Use finite-difference notation throughout or plot the recorded point derivatives. Locations: paper.pdf p. 12, Figure 1; `paper.tex:662-669`; `numerical_v4/checks/figures.py:74-95`.

## Minor items

1. **The economic policy is imposed rather than derived.** Utilities are defined, but no optimality or equilibrium condition selects the cutoff vector used in the results. The paper avoids claiming existence, so this is not a false theorem, but it leaves the comparative statics conditional on an arbitrary behavioral assignment. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 5-7 and 11, Sections 3.2-3.5 and 5; `paper.tex:254-271`, `paper.tex:355-365`, `paper.tex:387-414`, and `paper.tex:643-652`.

2. **The flagged information set is stronger than a Schedule 13D filing.** The introduction says that the market sees the filed stake and engagement intent. The formal tuple also makes the residual future purchase `Q^F` public, and the invariance proof uses `B^F+Q^F` to recover the signal. The paper needs an institutional interpretation for public `Q^F`, even if it keeps the assumption. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 2, 6, and 8-9; `paper.tex:87-95`, `paper.tex:333-337`, and `paper.tex:470-486`; appendix.pdf pp. 1-4.

3. **The threshold result is much narrower than the two-dial framing.** Condition 1 is `C_tau<=1` restated through the coefficient sum, not a primitive condition. Its sign is established only for four adjacent pairs on a 9.24 to 9.70 percent ladder at one frozen policy. This limitation should lead the contribution discussion, not appear after the general two-dial language. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 9 and 11-12, Sections 4.2 and 5; `paper.tex:523-529` and `paper.tex:643-703`.

4. **The empirical population does not map cleanly to Voice.** The model says only Voice crosses and a filing reveals engagement. E1 includes every initial 13D without an Item 4 engagement screen. It also uses the maximum reporting-person percentage in a joint filing, which need not equal the campaign group's stake when holdings are disjoint or nested. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 2, 5, and 14, Sections 1, 3.2, and 6.1; `paper.tex:87-95`, `paper.tex:254-258`, and `paper.tex:769-776`.

5. **The surviving empirical section does not measure market inference.** It measures filing stakes and delays. It has no price, order-flow, or liquidity outcome. The evidence can motivate the clock and the stake object, but it cannot support the title's market-inference claim. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 14-16, Section 6; `paper.tex:757-872`.

6. **The delay data need an outlier and validity discussion.** The result file reports two negative delays and 138 filings whose parsed trigger year predates 2021, including a 2010 trigger with a 2,870-business-day delay. The paper says it uses every measured delay but does not explain these observations or show that the clock summaries are robust to them. Locations: paper.pdf p. 16, Section 6.3; `paper.tex:839-870`; `empirics/output/e1_estimate.json`, keys `coverage.negative_delay` and `clock.by_trigger_year`.

7. **Paper and appendix numbering do not map.** The paper's Lemmas 1-4 and Theorems 1-2 do not have the same numbers or grouping in the appendix, and the paper gives no exact appendix pointers. This makes the proof artifact unnecessarily hard to audit. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 8-10, Section 4; appendix.pdf pp. 1, 6-7, and 10-15, Sections 1-2.

8. **Several set-equality readings need "almost surely."** For example, the threshold proof equates `W_tau=1` with no history being reclassified. Inclusion plus equal probability only proves that the reclassified set has zero mass. Locations: appendix.pdf p. 16, Theorem 2 and its proof; `proofs/02_garbling.tex:193-204` and `proofs/02_garbling.tex:388-407`.

9. **The novelty claims are categorical.** Statements that existing trading models never move pooled composition and that no adjacent literature separates the two margins need either a demonstrably exhaustive search or narrower wording. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 3-4, Sections 1 and 2.3-2.4; `paper.tex:133-138` and `paper.tex:195-226`.

10. **The PDFs have avoidable production defects.** All three figures introduce embedded Type 3 fonts, Figure 2's labels are crowded at print size, appendix p. 12 begins with an isolated carryover word, and paper p. 19 has only two bibliography entries. The TeX logs are otherwise clean. Locations: paper.pdf pp. 12, 14-15, and 19; appendix.pdf p. 12; `figures/fig1_sensitivity_factors.pdf`, `figures/fig2_who_gets_caught.pdf`, and `figures/e1_stake.pdf`.
