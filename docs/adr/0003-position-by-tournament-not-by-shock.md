# The position is chosen by a judged tournament, weighted by whitespace, fact-anchoring and December deliverability — not by whether a natural experiment exists

Several candidate positions differ in tools (monotone comparative statics, information design, tender-offer games) and in anchor (threshold margin, window margin, 13D-vs-13G choice, quantification). We generate them independently, judge them against full-text literature cards, and adversarially verify the winner. A causal design is preferred when one exists, but a position is never chosen *because* the Feb-2024 acceleration exists; a calibrated/quantified empirical leg is acceptable. Sidestep the competitor set rather than compete head-on.
